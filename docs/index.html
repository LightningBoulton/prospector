How your daily job email is built

The mandate it works to, and the exact gauntlet every job posting runs before it can appear in your inbox. Made so you can mark it up.

✍️
To request changes: reference the code next to anything — a pipeline stage (S4), a tunable knob (K6), or a specific word chip. e.g. "S4 / Group 2: add product marketing" or "K4: widen local window to 14 days."

The mandate

Every morning, surface fresh, genuinely-relevant job postings for you — across local, fully-remote, and contract work — filtered to your leadership profile, ranked by fit, with zero noise and no repeats.

It optimizes for four things, in tension:

  • Relevant — matches your level and your domain (operations, strategy, transformation, customer/CX, change, consulting), not just any open role.
  • Fresh — recently posted, so you're early rather than 200th in line.
  • Quiet — you only get an email on days something actually changed; nothing you've already seen is repeated.
  • Reachable — in the Salt Lake / Utah County area or US-remote; never a role you'd have to move for.

The decision tree

Follow one job posting from a company's careers page to your inbox. It has to clear every stage — fail one and it's out.

S1

Collect from the watch-lists 3 registries

Pull every open role from the companies being watched — one list per lane (see the three lanes below).

watching local employers ~71 remote employers 3 staffing firms
S2

Location gate is it reachable?

Keep the role only if it's in the Salt Lake / Utah County area or US-remote. On-site roles in other cities, and remote roles tied to another country, are dropped. (Each lane applies its own version — see below.)

✓ Utah-area or US-remote ✗ other-city on-site ✗ non-US remote
S3

Freshness gate how old?

Drop anything posted too long ago. Roles with no visible post-date are always kept (better to show than silently hide).

window Local & Remote: 7 days Contract/Staffing: 30 days
S4

Title match — your profile the main filter

The role's title is tested against your word-lists. It's kept only if the title contains at least one word from Group 1 and at least one from Group 2, and none of your exclude words. (The full lists are in K5–K7 below — this is your biggest lever.)

✓ "Director of Customer Experience" ✗ "Software Engineer" (excluded)
⚠️
Whole-word matching. It matches full words, not fragments — so change management in your list does not match a title that says change manager, and program management won't catch program manager. If you want those title variants, they each need their own entry. (We just added organizational change + change manager for exactly this reason.)
S5

Contract-only screen staffing lane only

In the Contract/Staffing lane, permanent placements are removed at the source, so only contract / contract-to-hire / temporary work comes through. The other two lanes skip this step.

✓ contract / temp / C2H ✗ permanent / direct-hire
S6

Fit scoring AI read

Each surviving new role is read against your background and scored 0–100 with a yes / maybe / no verdict and a one-line reason. Today this only ranks — nothing is dropped for a low score — so you see everything, best-fit first.

mode Rank (keep all, sort by fit) · alt Filter: hide "no"
S7

Compare to yesterday what changed

The list is diffed against the last run. This is what fills the What's changed section: brand-new roles, changed titles, and removed / filled ones. Everything still open also appears under All current matching roles.

✦ new ✎ changed title ✓ removed / filled
S8

Star the newest ⭐

A ⭐ is added to any role posted within the star window, so the very freshest jump out.

star window posted in the last 1 day
S9

Send only if something changed the quiet rule

If nothing is new, changed, or removed across any lane since the last run, no email is sent. A quiet inbox means nothing moved — not that the tool skipped a day.

The three lanes

One email, but the search runs in three separate lanes — each with its own company list, its own location rule, and its own section in the email.

📍

Local — Silicon Slopes

watch-list: local employers

Established local tech employers. Kept if the role is in the Salt Lake / Utah County area or US-remote.

Gate: Utah-area or US-remote
🌎

US-Remote

watch-list: ~71 remote employers

Remote-friendly national employers. Kept only if the role is genuinely remote and open to US-based candidates.

Gate: must be US-eligible remote
🤵‍💼

Contract / Staffing

watch-list: Aquent, Eliassen, Addison Group

Staffing firms, filtered to contract work. Same reach as Local (Utah-area or US-remote), with a wider 30-day freshness window.

Gate: Utah-area or US-remote · contract only
